How the Oracle predicts

Each team gets a rating from its season win rate (45%), last-5 form (35%) and average scoring margin (20%), plus a home-advantage bonus. The gap becomes home / draw / away chances, blended slightly towards typical results so early-season data can't make it overconfident. MMA picks use career records only.

For fun, not betting advice. It doesn't know about injuries, line-ups or travel.
